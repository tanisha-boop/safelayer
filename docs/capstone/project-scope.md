# SafeLayer Project Scope

## Project Purpose

SafeLayer is a full-stack web application for improving operational traceability and trust in organizations with distributed or field-based work.

The system connects operational activities with evidence, verification, risk assessment, review, and auditability.

## In Scope

### Field Operations

- field activity creation
- assignment
- activity lifecycle
- operational status tracking

### Evidence

- evidence upload
- activity-linked evidence
- file validation
- file hashing
- verification workflow

### RouteProof

- field check-in
- GPS capture
- field check-out
- location-based operational evidence

### FieldLedger

- collection records
- payment collection
- remittance
- remittance items
- verification
- reconciliation-oriented workflows

### InvoiceGuard

- invoice records
- invoice verification
- invoice review
- review resolution

### Trust & Risk

- trust assessment
- operational risk information
- decision-support workflow

### Human Review

- review queue
- review state
- priority
- entity association
- resolution workflow

### Compliance

- compliance requirements
- status
- expiry tracking
- evidence association

### Notifications

- user notifications
- unread state
- organization association
- entity association

### Reporting

- operational metrics
- activity performance
- evidence assurance
- review workload
- financial control information
- risk and trust information

### Auditability

- auditable operational events
- traceability of important actions

## Out of Scope

SafeLayer does not attempt to provide:

- medical diagnosis
- autonomous safety decisions
- legal advice
- financial advice
- unrestricted surveillance
- direct banking infrastructure
- a replacement for an organization's complete ERP
- autonomous approval of high-impact decisions

## AI Boundary

Where intelligent analysis is used or expanded, it is treated as decision support.

Authorized humans remain responsible for final operational decisions.

## Intended Users

The architecture supports roles such as:

- Owner
- Administrator
- Manager
- Field Worker
- Finance
- Auditor

## Intended Organizational Context

The platform is designed for organizations with distributed operations, including potential use cases involving:

- collections
- delivery
- field sales
- service operations
- inspections
- property maintenance
- construction operations
- compliance workflows

The core architecture is intentionally broader than a single industry.
